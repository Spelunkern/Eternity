# 06-combat integration assessment

Scope: investigate composition of the existing user attack/skill scheduler with the retained mob and world owners. No runtime implementation was committed: the available owners cannot yet express the original hit transition without inventing state or behavior. Assessed source checkpoint: `18a38914b8480c832f33aa249b79446419c03c8e`. The report commit is separate and contains no gameplay changes.

Evidence: `ps_game.exe` CUser::UpdateAttack 0048ca50 reaches normal CUser::AttackMob 0044ce10; that caller invokes DamageParty at 0044cf19, Damage at 0044cf3f, then stop/death on a lethal result. The skill AttackMob coordinator 00450cf0 routes selected attacks to the skill hit implementation 0044fd90. CMob::Damage 00490cf0 / 00490ee0 writes HP, MP, SP, optional hit count, primary and secondary state, and corpse deadline; it dispatches event 3 before logging and ledger insertion, and may retarget afterwards. Existing `mob_combat_user_hit` implements the credit and damage segment with explicit projections; `user_skill_runtime_attack_mob` routes to an abstract effect; neither computes the original hit or owns the live transition. See `reports/parallel/01-mob-damage.md` for original-execution evidence and offsets.

Integration requirements:

- Extend the existing `MobRuntime` and definition/linked owners with authoritative access to hit-enable +258, hit-count +25c, secondary state +128, definition long-delay selector +6c, and log selectors +284 / linked +c84 byte +1c. HP, MP, SP, status, state stack, death time and mob-owned damage ledger already have runtime owners. Establish exact lifecycle/initialization and callback visibility; a local copied projection with delayed writeback would lose mutations observed during event, clock, logging and retarget callbacks.
- Recover and bind the full normal AttackMob 0044ce10 calculation, guards, random draws, effect and packet ordering, and the selected skill hit 0044fd90. `SetAttack` stats are explicitly not hit calculation. Supply actual party identity and CUser aggro bonus +1230 from their existing owners, including membership reload after party credit.
- Bind real FSM event 3, tick, mob damage log and ordinary/stacked retarget services, plus `mob_combat_stop(Actor&)` and `mob_death` with its real reward providers. Keep the actual mob, ledger, actor and party alive and serialized throughout callbacks. Do not substitute no-op success providers.
- Root assembly must call the existing zone scheduler using the retained `MobPrimaryZoneLease`, actual ZoneUsers and actor leases, and connect `UserSkillRuntimeAttackWorld` and the real effect/spending providers. A zone lease alone does not protect recycled actor identity. Preserve skill cooldown/effect/spend ordering and do not rerun a coordinator prefix when continuing a partial path.

Limitations and next dependency: normal player-to-mob combat cannot be connected correctly until the missing canonical mob fields and original normal hit calculation are recovered. Prioritize those two dependencies, then test one actual retained actor attacking an actual pooled mob through the scheduler, including nonlethal and lethal hits and real death services. This assessment does not establish client playability.
