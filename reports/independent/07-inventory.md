## Follow-up: scene transport failures

Code commit: `c6d252034ba1b1704bd404a8857e6b66ced9ef1b`. `VerticalSceneServices::transport_failure` is a mandatory noexcept failure owner: root must record the fault and initiate connection/world teardown, retain the scene through the callback and handle partial group delivery. It receives a distinct unavailable-buffer indication or the caught exception for self send, group-state or group-send failure. Party lock and sender buffer references are released on error; an enqueue that throws must not have accepted its reserved reference, and an allocator that throws must retain responsibility for any unreturned storage. Buffer-free failure is reported without attempting a second release. The party lock's `unlock` remains a required noexcept provider.

`WorldChannel::send` posts asynchronously and becomes inert after disconnect; successful return is not delivery confirmation. Later socket queue overflow, encryption or write failures are handled by the WorldServer connection close path, not reported synchronously by this interface. Root must retain connection lifecycle state and bind the mandatory failure owner to an allocation-safe disconnect/teardown path; a shared channel acceptance/completion interface is needed if it must distinguish an already-closed channel or obtain delivery acknowledgment. The scene does not claim it can observe those outcomes.
