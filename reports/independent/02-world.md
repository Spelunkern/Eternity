## Integration interface and limitations

The root supplies actual `ServerMaps`, a single already bound `MobPool`, `MobZoneServicesInputs` retaining all real backends, transports, and `VerticalWorldPorts`. The ports must construct and attach retained obelisk records/router against the provided primary authority, provide the real complete per-zone frame and reset clock. Bind this frame to `vertical_mobs_idle_frame` with the same pool, Init ports, linked-mob world and obelisk owners used by the zone factory. The callback runs under the primary authority's serialized lease. `start()` is single-use. On startup exception it attempts drain while preserving the original exception; if cleanup fails or leases defer it, retain the world and retry `shutdown()`. Stop producers and release leases before shutdown; do not destroy an initialized authority before drain completes. Frame exceptions likewise require explicit shutdown. All borrowed backends and the Init ports must outlive the authority and pool.

The scheduler calls `VerticalWorld::frame` once per intended world frame; it invokes the callback once per primary in map order under a lease. The callback is the sole owner of that primary zone’s regular, obelisk and linked population pass: do not separately call `vertical_mobs_idle_frame`, `RuntimeZone::update_mob_population`, `MobPrimaryZones::tick`, or linked respawn for the same zone and frame. 05-mobs reports original `ps_game.exe` `CZone::UpdateMobIdle` 00421a00 runs idle traversal, then `UpdateMobDeath`, then `UpdateMobRespawn`; the frame callback must preserve this sequence. `VerticalWorld` performs no population update before the callback. Within the respawn pass, `CZone::UpdateMobRespawn` 0041d240 orders regular, primary obelisk, then linked updates. The 02-world test verifies delegation and absence of an earlier respawn; it does not execute the 05-mobs idle/death implementation or independently validate that sequence. The original scheduler interval and ordering relative to other frame work remain unverified; no fixed interval or complete ordering is claimed. Exceptions may leave a partial pass; do not blindly replay the frame. Instance-zone frames, live AI and world scheduling, actual transport/Lua/KC providers, admission and main-loop wiring are still missing. The lifecycle test uses real retained primary/registry/population owners with fixture services; it does not establish full production provider integration or client playability. Next dependency: integrate real world providers and determine the original caller's startup/frame/shutdown scheduling, then wire this assembly into the root alongside instance-zone lifecycle.

The `vertical_mobs` sources are not present in this branch, so a real combined callback cannot be compiled or validated here. After integrating 05-mobs, construct its real idle-frame provider using the same retained `MobPrimaryZones::mob_pool()`, mob Init ports, canonical AI/death providers, and zone-specific linked and obelisk services already owned by the world. The 05-mobs provider is responsible for the full reported `CZone::UpdateMobIdle` 00421a00 order: idle traversal, `UpdateMobDeath`, then `UpdateMobRespawn`. Its respawn pass must use the same actual zone/pool and existing primary obelisk and linked population owners. Do not also schedule any of those passes through `VerticalWorld`, a second mob scheduler, or direct `MobPrimaryZones::tick` for that zone/frame.

Integration validation must compose the actual 05-mobs implementation and actual retained zone/lease with recording real-provider boundaries, prove idle → death → one regular/obelisk/linked respawn pass, and verify zone/actor retention and deferred drain while the callback is active. Existing `vertical-world-unit` has a fixture callback that calls population update only: it does not establish the idle/death order and must not be cited as an original-frame oracle. The real callback signature, full scheduler cadence, instance-zone scheduling and remaining runtime providers must be checked against the integrated 05-mobs interface; they are not reconstructed by this report.
