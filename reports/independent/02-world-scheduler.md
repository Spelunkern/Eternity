# World scheduler evidence and integration blocker

Evidence commit: `5c7f9176a2128142f2269f2a62db4b4bd6e123d9`. This is an original-execution fixture and research handoff, not a production scheduler. No runtime scheduler or synthetic provider was added.

## Original executable evidence

- `ps_game.exe` `CWorldThread::CWorldThread` 00407100, instructions 0040718e–004071f1, initializes its timer DWORDs to zero, including user attack +74, applied skill/effects +78/+7c/+80, mob attack +84, mob idle +88 and wait-zone promotion +a4. These are original constructor writes, not proposed startup guesses.
- `CWorldThread::Run` 00407330 uses a shutdown event and samples the tick for `Update`, then waits for the balance of 250 ms when work takes less than 250 ms. This is the thread loop, not the mob attack or idle interval.
- `CWorldThread::Update` 00407390: the unsigned due comparison at 004073a1 promotes waiting zones through the original active thread list, then sets +a4 to `now+100` at 00407464. It traverses the active zone list for `CZone::UpdatePackets` 004215e0 on every update. Then the separately due passes traverse the list in this order: user attack (+74, `now+100`), applied skill (+78, `now+1000`), effect tick (+7c, `now+3000`), effect (+80, `now+5000`), mob attack followed by NPC attack for each zone (+84, `now+500`), then mob idle followed by `CZone::UpdateTrab` 004226b0 for each zone (+88, `now+1000`). Further original passes follow at 0040767d and beyond. The unsigned comparisons and each deadline write happen once per due update; no catch-up loop exists, arithmetic wraps at 32 bits. `CZone::UpdateMobIdle` 00421a00 itself owns idle traversal, death cleanup and the respawn pass per 05-mobs evidence.
- `CZone::UpdatePackets` 004215e0 traverses its zone-wait users, then active zone users, then reset-position processing under original list ownership. `CWorld::UpdatePackets` 0040bf90 and `WorldAdmission::update_packets()` cover connected/selection/world-wait transitions and are not a substitute for this per-zone pass.

## Original execution fixture

`tools/vertical_world_scheduler_oracle.py` executes the actual instructions in 00407390 up to 0040767d with two actual intrusive active-zone nodes, observes 160 combinations of now/attack/idle deadlines including equality and near-wrap, and records actual post-deadlines and packet/mob-attack/NPC-attack/mob-idle/trap call order in `tests/data/vertical-world-scheduler-oracle.tsv`. Zone bodies and OS critical-section calls are intercepted as observed ports; wait-zone promotion is suppressed by its not-due deadline, and user-attack/skill/effect deadlines are similarly not due in these cases. The fixture does not execute complete zone bodies, constructor, later passes, dynamic queue changes, or the full thread. Constructor initialization above is from Ghidra disassembly. The oracle verifies the immutable executable SHA-256 via `original_oracle.py`; it was regenerated twice and the outputs matched byte for byte.

## Why production composition is blocked

`VerticalWorld::frame` currently visits permanent primary maps in map order, calling a whole idle frame once per primary; it does not own `CWorldThread`'s ordered active/wait zone lists, and cannot run an original **per-zone** `UpdateTrab` immediately after each idle callback while admitting dynamic instances. `MobPrimaryZones` keeps its `ZoneRegistry` private and supplies only primary leases. Thus placing timers around `VerticalWorld::frame` would omit instances and wait-zone promotion, substitute map order for thread-list order, and skip or reorder the actual packet, attack, NPC, skill/effect and trap passes. `WorldAdmission` in this branch owns zone-wait lists privately; main commit `25d85e2` adds `update_zone_wait_packets` (coordinator reports 1,024 original sequences plus removal traversal), but it is not integrated here; its world-level `update_packets()` is not the missing `CZone::UpdatePackets`. The 05-mobs idle implementation and production attack, NPC, trap and packet providers are not present in this branch. Supplying no-op callbacks or a second thread list would fabricate the requested behavior and compete with the retained owners. The pending admission-service blocker and acknowledged ingress pause are documented in `reports/independent/02-world-admission.md`.

Smallest integration dependency: expose a single retained thread-owned active/wait zone traversal and promotion authority over the **same** `MobPrimaryZones`/`ZoneRegistry`, including dynamic zone leases, original live cursor semantics and shutdown/drain pinning; expose the existing admission owner's zone-wait traversal under the same executor and actual user lifetime pins. Bind real zone packet/reset-position, user/mob/NPC attack, skill/effect, 05-mobs idle, trap and remaining due-pass providers. Only then assemble the original ordered passes and timers against those owners, keeping ingress pause acknowledged and the original admission lists. Preserve the initial zero deadlines and single `now+interval` write after each pass. Do not run the current all-primary `VerticalWorld::frame` alongside an integrated 05-mobs idle pass. Validate full and reentrant traversal, waiting promotion, wrap, actual admission/zone membership and shutdown against original execution before claiming the scheduler integrated.

## Extended original-execution follow-up

Evidence commit `f14e3f0312891c9da914bfacd5db3ce6f8e7f34a` adds `tools/vertical_world_scheduler_extended_oracle.py`, `tests/data/vertical-world-scheduler-extended.tsv` and `tests/vertical_world_scheduler_extended_test.py`. The earlier fixture remains unchanged. The extended fixture executes `ps_game.exe` `CWorldThread::Update` 00407390 through 00407c00 over original intrusive zone links, with real `SSyncList<CZoneLink>::RemoveFront` 00408140 for wait promotion and original `SSyncList<CZone>::Remove` 00407fa0 or `CWorldThread::AddZone` 004072b0 for mutations triggered during a packet boundary. It observes 2,496 combinations of ten timer deadlines, equality/preceding/following values, unsigned wrap, active and wait membership, current/future removal and append. It records calls for packet, user attack, apply-skill, effect tick, effect, mob/NPC attack, mob idle/trap, NPC idle, item, quest, kill-count boundary, world-send and conditional net-send. Actual list links, counts, and disjoint membership are checked after each original execution.

This fixture does **not** execute the called gameplay bodies: zone operations, kill-count body and OS locks are intercepted observation ports, with mutation stubs invoking the actual original list functions. For `now == 0xffffffff`, all DWORD deadlines compare due, so execution stops at 00407765 before the weather pass rather than asserting an invented way to suppress later timers. At other ticks, weather, guild-rank, war and instance-expiry timers are intentionally not due; execution stops at 00407c00 before final lock release. Their due paths and real production scheduler remain unverified. The fixture does not implement or use a second zone registry.
